# Media Buying Framework Agreement

> **Demonstration document — entirely fictional.**
> Zava Media, Adventure Works Travel and the media owners named here are imaginary
> entities. This text has no legal value and reproduces no real contract.

**Reference:** ZM-ADV-005-2026
**Between:** Zava Media SAS, hereinafter "the Agency", acting as **agent** (mandataire)
**And:** Adventure Works Travel SpA, hereinafter "the Advertiser", the principal
**Advertiser ID:** ADV-005

---

## Article 1 — Purpose and nature of the mandate

**1.1** The Advertiser appoints the Agency to buy advertising space in its name and on
its behalf.

**1.2** The Agency acts exclusively as an **agent**. It does not buy space on its own
account for resale to the Advertiser.

**1.3** For purchases made in France, the parties comply with French Law no. 93-122 of
29 January 1993 on transparency in advertising, and with its implementing regulations
covering programmatic buying.

## Article 2 — Term

From 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026.

## Article 3 — Scope

**3.1 Brands covered:** Adventure Works Cruises, Adventure Works Breaks.

**3.2 Markets covered:** Italy, France, Spain, United Kingdom.

## Article 4 — Agency remuneration

**4.1** Fixed quarterly fee of **EUR 72,000**.

**4.2** The Agency's remuneration is **exclusive of any other**. The Agency declares that
it receives no benefit, rebate, discount or service from media owners in connection with
the Advertiser's campaigns that is not passed through to the Advertiser in full.

## Article 5 — Direct invoicing and statement of mandate

**5.1** For purchases falling under Article 1.3, the media owner invoices the Advertiser
**directly**. The Agency does not interpose itself in the invoicing flow.

**5.2** The Agency shall provide the Advertiser with a quarterly **statement of mandate**
setting out, by market, media owner and channel:
- the gross amount negotiated,
- rebates obtained,
- the net amount actually paid to the media owner,
- planned volumes and delivered volumes,
- the delivery variance observed.

**5.3** The statement of mandate is delivered within **30 days** of the close of the
quarter. Failure to deliver it suspends the payability of that quarter's fees.

## Article 6 — Delivery variances

**6.1** Any delivery variance exceeding **5 %** for a given market and quarter must be
**reported and explained** in the statement of mandate, whether the variance is positive
or negative.

**6.2** An unreported variance constitutes a breach of mandate within the meaning of
Article 8.

**6.3** Over-delivery is not billable. It accrues to the Advertiser.

**6.4** Under-delivery exceeding **5 %** gives rise to compensation in kind in the
following quarter, on the same market.

## Article 7 — Audit right

**7.1** The Advertiser has an **annual audit right**, exercised by an independent third
party of its choosing, covering performance of the mandate over the preceding twelve
months.

**7.2** The Agency shall make available, within **15 days** of the request: purchase
orders, media owner invoices, proof of broadcast, delivery statements and the detail of
rebates obtained.

**7.3** Refusal or delay in disclosure constitutes a breach within the meaning of
Article 8.

## Article 8 — Breach of mandate

**8.1** Any established breach of the transparency obligations in Articles 4, 5, 6 and 7
entitles the Advertiser to terminate with immediate effect, without notice or indemnity.

**8.2** Sums wrongfully retained by the Agency shall be repaid, together with interest at
the statutory rate.

## Article 9 — Invoicing and payment of fees

**9.1** Fees are invoiced quarterly in arrears.

**9.2** Delivered volumes are reconciled monthly between the media owner's delivery
statement and the invoicing statement. Any volume delivered and not invoiced after
**90 days** is reported to the Advertiser.

**9.3** Payment terms: 30 days end of month.

## Article 10 — Termination

Six months' notice, save where Article 8 applies.
