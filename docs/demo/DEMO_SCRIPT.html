Zava Media — demo script

A fictional media agency, five advertisers, and one question that neither the data nor the contract can answer alone. This script gives the order of the screens, the clicks, what to say and the wording for the Zava IQ part, so the audience understands the context of what they are about to see — before they see it.

1. The storyline

“Did we over-deliver Contoso Mobility in Spain in Q3 — and does their contract provide for compensation?”

The figure is calculated in Fabric, in DAX, on the Lakehouse. The clause is retrieved by Foundry from the contract corpus. The language model routes, cites and phrases — it never calculates, and never turns a clause into an invented rule.

Advertiser × market × quarterGap vs planContractConsequence
Contoso Mobility × Spain × Q3+12.00%ADV-001 art. 6.1–6.2, 6.4The excess cannot be billed and a compensation credit of 50% of the media value of the excess is due within 45 days, without the client asking. No netting across markets.
Litware Retail × United Kingdom × Q3+11.00%ADV-004 art. 6.1–6.3Nothing. Compensation, credit and carry-over are excluded; the agency cannot bill the excess either.
Fabrikam Beauty × Italy × Q3−8.00%ADV-002 art. 6.2A penalty of 2% of the net media budget, owed by the agency, automatically, without formal notice.
Three gaps of the same size. Three opposite consequences. The numbers say how much; the contract says what to do. Neither is enough on its own.

2. Before the demo

The day before

  • Fabric capacity running (a paused capacity breaks everything, with no clear message).
  • Test suite green: python -m pytest tests/ -q.
  • Full rehearsal in the browser you will use on stage (regular Edge, already signed in to the right tenant).
  • Optional: regenerate the recorded evidence for the IQ page with python -m foundry.capture_iq_dossiers, then read its output. An incomplete capture is not ready for a customer.

One hour before

  • python deploy_all.py --warmup — pay the cold start off stage.
  • Open the application URL (application_url in state.json) and sign in.
  • Ask the live question from scene 3 once: it wakes up the Fabric + Foundry chain.
  • Second tab open on /diagnostic (not in the navigation, reachable without signing in).

Five minutes before

  • Browser zoom at 100%, full-screen window, theme chosen.
  • Go back to the home page; close the assistant panel if it still holds rehearsal answers.
  • Notifications off.

What to say while it runs

What to show

From the assistant’s follow-up suggestions: “Which media owners carry the campaigns in the off-plan market?” — recorded answer, graph traversal.

What to say
The semantic model answers “how much”. The ontology answers “what is connected to what”. Here the advertiser can only be reached through its brands: there is no direct shortcut, on purpose. That forces a real graph traversal rather than a disguised join.
It is also what lets us check the scope: which campaigns really belong to Contoso in Spain.
5Contracts — “Five master agreements, three regimes”2 min
What to show

A page deliberately without a single number. The summaries are captions, not evidence.

Click — live question

Click the Litware Retail row: a question on the text alone (≈ 30–40 s).

What to say
The right to compensation is not in the data warehouse. It is in the contract. If I added KPIs to this page to balance it, I would destroy the point.
And it has to be provable: French transparency law (loi Sapin) requires the agency to show how a billed figure was produced.
Do not use the prepared questions “What do the contracts provide for…” or “What deadlines do those clauses run on?” here: their current recordings are poor (invented names). Prefer clicking a contract row, which runs live.
6Billing — “What was never billed”2 min
What to show
  • Two panels side by side: 6 incomplete billing lines (campaign × media owner grain) and 2 campaigns affected (campaign grain), for €649,159.
  • “Ask the question” button: the claim window, read from the contract.
What to say
Both numbers are right. They do not answer the same question. A campaign bought from three media owners makes three lines: ask “how many campaigns” and get a line count, and you are wrong by a factor of three without any alert firing.
The deadline does not come from the data: Litware grants 120 days (art. 9.2). After that the claim is time-barred — here on 28 January 2027.

8Architecture — “The boundary rule”1 min 30
What to show

The deployed chain: Lakehouse, Eventhouse, ontology, semantic model and Data Agent on the Fabric side; supervisor, contracts agent and vector store on the Foundry side; the Fabric App on top.

What to say
One rule: the data world stays on the data side. Measures, aggregation and semantics live in Fabric. Foundry orchestrates, retrieves the clause and drafts. It never reimplements a metric.
The Data Agent is a tool — you delegate a question to it and it returns an answer. The contract corpus is a knowledge source — you retrieve text and the agent reasons over it.

7. Challenge questions for the room

Deliberate traps. The expected answers are locked in by the test suite.

Audience questionExpected answer
Contoso over-delivers by 12% in Spain. Do we bill it?No: above 5%, the excess cannot be billed (6.1). Above 10%, a credit of 50% of the media value of the excess is due within 45 days, without the client asking (6.2).
Litware over-delivers by 11%. Same treatment?No: no compensation, whatever the gap (6.1–6.2). And the agency cannot bill the excess (6.3).
Can we offset Spain against an under-delivered Contoso market?No: gaps are assessed per market and per quarter, with no netting across markets (6.4).
Fabrikam under-delivers by 8% in Italy. Make-good in kind?Not at 8%: a penalty of 2% of the net media budget, automatically (6.2). Make-good in kind only adds on above 10% (6.3–6.4).

How many campaigns were not fully billed?Two campaigns — but six lines at campaign × media owner grain. €649,159 missing, claimable for 120 days (Litware, 9.2), until 28 Jan 2027.
