# Media Buying Framework Agreement

> **Demonstration document — entirely fictional.**
> Zava Media, Contoso Mobility and the media owners named here are imaginary entities.
> This text has no legal value and reproduces no real contract.

**Reference:** ZM-ADV-001-2026
**Between:** Zava Media SAS, hereinafter "the Agency"
**And:** Contoso Mobility SA, hereinafter "the Advertiser"
**Advertiser ID:** ADV-001

---

## Article 1 — Purpose

The Agency shall plan, buy, monitor and reconcile media investments on behalf of the
Advertiser across all brands and markets listed in Article 3.

## Article 2 — Term

This agreement runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026, renewable by tacit
agreement for successive one-year periods.

## Article 3 — Scope

**3.1 Brands covered:** Contoso EV, Contoso Fleet.

**3.2 Markets covered:** Germany, Spain, France, Italy, United Kingdom.

**3.3** Any additional market or brand requires a written amendment.

## Article 4 — Agency remuneration

**4.1** The Agency is remunerated by a fixed annual fee. No commission is charged on
media volume.

**4.2** The Agency receives no remuneration, direct or indirect, from media owners in
connection with the Advertiser's campaigns.

## Article 5 — Volume commitment

**5.1** The Advertiser commits to a minimum annual net media investment of
**EUR 8,000,000** across all markets.

**5.2** Quarterly media plans approved by the Advertiser constitute the **contracted
volume** for the purposes of Article 6.

## Article 6 — Delivery variances

**6.1 Over-delivery.** Any delivery exceeding the contracted volume of a quarterly media
plan by more than **5 %** is **not billable** to the Advertiser. The excess volume
accrues to the Advertiser at no charge.

**6.2 Compensation credit ("make-good").** Where over-delivery exceeds **10 %** for a
given market and quarter, the Agency shall issue a **compensation credit** equal to
**50 % of the media value of the excess**, valued at the contracted net rate of the
channel concerned. The credit is applicable against the following quarter.

The credit shall be issued within **45 days** of the close of the quarter concerned,
**without any prior request from the Advertiser**.

**6.3 Under-delivery.** Any under-delivery exceeding **3 %** of the contracted volume
shall be compensated in kind — equivalent advertising space on the same channel and
market — within **60 days**.

**6.4** Variances are assessed **per market and per quarter**. Offsetting between markets
is not permitted.

## Article 7 — Transparency and audit

**7.1** The Agency shall report quarterly, by market and by media owner, the gross, net
and net-net amounts of media purchased.

**7.2** The Advertiser has an annual audit right, exercised by an independent third party
of its choosing, covering the preceding twelve months. The Agency shall make purchase
documentation available within 30 days of the request.

## Article 8 — Year-end rebates

**8.1** All rebates, discounts and benefits granted by media owners in connection with
the Advertiser's campaigns are **passed through in full to the Advertiser**.

**8.2** Pass-through occurs no later than 31 March of the following year.

## Article 9 — Invoicing and payment

**9.1** Invoicing is monthly in arrears, based on volumes actually delivered and verified.

**9.2** Any delivered service must be invoiced within a maximum of **90 days** following
the end of the month of broadcast. After that period, the Agency may no longer claim
payment for it.

**9.3** Payment terms: 45 days end of month.

## Article 10 — Termination

Either party may terminate on six months' written notice sent by recorded delivery.
